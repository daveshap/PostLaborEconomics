# Post-Labor Economics

![Post-Labor Economics](PLE%20banner.jpg)

This is a public repository for all my research going into Post-Labor Economics, which will be used for several books. This research is too important to keep locked away. Furthermore, for the sake of rapid dissemination, robust consensus, and dialectical discussions, this repo is open source.

**Website: [daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics](https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/)**

## Start here

- **Reading *Labor Zero*?** The book's free companion is at [daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/laborzero](https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/laborzero/): the full Appendix and Bibliography, the four graphics from the front of the book, and three supplementary papers.
- **The research library:** [all 103 reports, grouped by theme](https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/research/).
- **Charts and memes:** [41 charts with captions](https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/charts/) and [27 memes](https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/memes/).

## Repo structure

The `/Research` folder is the meat and potatoes of this repository. It contains many PDF reports generated by Deep Research tools. This is research directed by humans and automatically carried out by machine, representing the equivalent of thousands of hours worth of work.

This material is to be used as research tools in the drafting of several books, but this repository is extremely valuable in its own right. Feel free to copy the PDFs into NotebookLM or other tools of your choice to discuss.

| Folder or file | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [`Research/`](Research/) | The research reports, as PDFs (103). |
| [`Research_txt/`](Research_txt/) | Plain-text copies of the reports, for NotebookLM and chatbots. |
| [`Household Income/`](Household%20Income/) | The reports on household income, dividends and ownership, gathered in one place. |
| [`Charts/`](Charts/) | 41 charts and figures. |
| [`Memes/`](Memes/) | 27 memes. |
| [`docs/`](docs/) | The website (GitHub Pages). `docs/laborzero/` is the *Labor Zero* companion; its address is printed in the book, so it never moves. |
| `generate_pdf_pages.py` | Puts new reports from `Research/` on the website. |
| `convert_pdfs_to_txt.py` | Makes the plain-text copies in `Research_txt/`. |

Files here keep their paths, so links to them keep working.
